KEY FINDINGS
------------
tk=64 (VALID):
* 1x4 (B-first) beats 1x4a (A-first) in EVERY config: ratio 0.71-0.92
  (1x4 ~25-30% faster at the largest tile BM128/BN256).
* 1x4 peak = 244.8 TFLOPS @ BM64/BN256/B4096; best tile is BM64/BN256 (240-245 TF).
  (Backup name "251T" ~ this peak; run-to-run variance ~2-3%.)
* 1x4 worst tile = BM128/BN256 (164-211 TF). Throughput sags at B=32768
  (occupancy / L2 pressure) across all tiles.
* BM=64 tiles are the sweet spot for this core (keeps all 4 waves productive).

tk=64 vs tk=128 (both VALID, 1x4, TFLOPS @ B=16384):
  BM64 /BN128 : tk64 224.1  >  tk128 217.6
  BM64 /BN256 : tk64 240.6  >  tk128 218.9
  BM128/BN128 : tk64 218.6  >  tk128 168.9
  BM128/BN256 : tk64 197.2  <  tk128 213.8   <- tk128 wins ONLY here
* Same pattern as moe_gemm_splitk: a larger TILE_K helps only at the largest tile
  (BM128/BN256); elsewhere tk=64 wins (tk=128 doubles A-LDS -> lower occupancy).
* 1x4 still beats 1x4a at tk=128 (ratio 0.62-0.92).
